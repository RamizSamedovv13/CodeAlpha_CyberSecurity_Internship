## Factual vs fictional content
- General patterns (urgency, impersonation, lookalike domains, malicious links/attachments, credential harvesting, reporting and password-change advice) reflect themes in the guidance above.
- All brands, domains, emails, numbers and scenarios (Northbridge Bank, Cloudly, Parcelo, `.example` domains) are **fictional training content**, not real incidents.
- No statistics are cited. Add figures only with a direct source link.
- The sources were not programmatically re-fetched while writing; verify details against the live pages.
